put a picture in · tag it · get somewhere to actually buy it

Put up a photo of an outfit, then answer four questions about what you can see in it. The answers are matched against a directory of independent shops, resale sites and markets, sorted by how near you are. No chain stores in the list. Nothing here costs anything.
