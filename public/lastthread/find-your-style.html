find your style

put a picture in · tag it · get somewhere to actually buy it

Upload a photo of an outfit. Yours, one you saved, a runway shot, or a single garment. Press read, and it names the garments, the cloth it can see and the subgenres the look belongs to. Those tags are matched against a directory of independent shops, resale sites and markets, sorted by how near you are. No chain stores in the list.

1. the picture

nothing yet

The picture is sent to be read once, when you press the button, and is not stored. Reading needs a mythefeed.com account.

2. the subgenre

Ticked by the reading, or tap them yourself. Hover for what each one means.

3. where you are

Location is asked for by your browser, used once to sort the list, and never stored or sent anywhere.

where to find it

    your shopping list

    what to look for, not what to buy

      Every shop here is real and independently run. They are listed by city, not street address, because addresses and hours change and we cannot check them from here. Look the shop up before you travel.
