find your style

put a picture in · tag it · get somewhere to actually buy it

Put up a photo of an outfit if you like, then describe what you can see in it, or answer four questions instead. What you say is matched against a directory of independent shops, resale sites and markets, sorted by how near you are. No chain stores in the list, and nothing here costs anything.
