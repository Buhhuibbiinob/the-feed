1947 · silhouette · Paris

The New Look was a fabric argument before it was a shape

filed by amaiya · 12 minutes ago

plate 01
Bar suit, 1947

The skirt on Dior's Bar suit uses somewhere between twelve and twenty metres of cloth, depending on whether you count the underskirt. Three years earlier that much fabric was rationed. The shape comes from the inside: a boned waist tape, hip padding, a horsehair interlining at the peplum. The jacket was made by a corsetière before it was draped.

1962–1975 · subculture · Kingston

Rude boy tailoring, and what a short trouser actually signalled

filed by m.okonjo · yesterday

plate 02
Kingston, c. 1966

The short trouser is usually called a mod import. Jamaican tailors were already cutting above the ankle, because the shoe cost more than the trousers and needed to show. The suit went to Brixton with the music. The length stayed. By 1979 two-tone bands were wearing it on record sleeves.

The white basting stitch, left in on purpose

filed by studio_b · 2 days ago

plate 03
Exposed basting

A basting stitch holds cloth in place while the real seam is sewn, then it gets pulled out. Margiela left his in, in white thread, at the shoulder. Four tacks, visible on the outside. The garment shows how it was put together and roughly when.

1920s · cloth & dye · Kano

Indigo pits, and the blue that rubs off deliberately

filed by amaiya · 4 days ago

plate 04
Kofar Mata dye pits

Indigo from a fermentation vat gets its depth one dip at a time. Twenty dips is darker than five, and costs more. The blue that comes off on your skin was the evidence of how many dips you had paid for. Modern finishing treats that as a fault and washes it out.

1973 · houses · Versailles

The Battle of Versailles, counted properly

filed by r.velez · 5 days ago

plate 05
Grand Divertissement

Five American designers showed against five French houses at Versailles in 1973. Most accounts are about the clothes. The part that changed things was the walk: the American half used ten Black models, moved them to music, and built almost no set. The French side had a Cinderella carriage. Runway shows have worked the American way since.

1996–2004 · patterns · Tokyo

Flat pattern as a moral position

filed by studio_b · last week

plate 06
Two-dimensional cut

Draping starts at the body and cuts away. Flat pattern starts at a rectangle of cloth and tries not to waste any of it. That is where the low armhole comes from, and the sleeve that reads as a fold, and the coat that looks wrong on a hanger.
