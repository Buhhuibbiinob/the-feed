1947 · silhouette · Paris

filed by dyamanite · sources at the foot

Dior showed the Corolle line in February 1947. It is remembered for the hemline. The more useful thing to look at is the inside of the jacket: a boned waist tape, hip padding, horsehair at the peplum. The skirt takes twelve to twenty metres of cloth depending on how you count. Paris had been rationing cloth three years before. That is where the argument came from.

SPRING 1947

note

The yardage figure varies between sources, twelve to twenty metres, depending on whether the underskirt is counted. Both numbers are cited; the range is the honest way to give it.
