1947 · silhouette · Paris

filed by amaiya · corrected twice · sources at the foot

Dior's February 1947 collection is remembered as a hemline. It was a foundation. The Bar jacket sits on a boned waist tape and hip padding cut from the same block a corsetière would have used before the war; the skirt takes fifteen or so metres of cloth in a city that had been counting coupons three years earlier. The shape is structural, and the scandal was arithmetic.

SPRING 1947
