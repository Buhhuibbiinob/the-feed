find your style

put a picture in · tag it · get somewhere to actually buy it

Upload a photo of an outfit. Yours, one you saved, a runway shot, or a single garment. Tag what it is. The site matches those tags against a directory of independent shops, resale sites and markets, and sorts the results by how near you are. No chain stores in the list.

1. the picture

nothing yet

The photo is read in your browser and is not uploaded. Automatic detection of what is in it is coming soon. It needs a server, which this site does not have yet.

2. the subgenre

Tap everything that applies. Hover for what each one means.

3. where you are

Location is asked for by your browser, used once to sort the list, and never stored or sent anywhere.

where to find it

    your shopping list

    what to look for, not what to buy

      Every shop here is real and independently run. They are listed by city, not street address, because addresses and hours change and we cannot check them from here. Look the shop up before you travel.
